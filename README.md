COP31 collapses in Antalya after the longest climate summit on record; war escalates at the Strait of Hormuz as Iran strikes US bases in Bahrain and hits two tankers; a Ukrainian drone sets a Russian cargo ship ablaze off Finland; an Indonesian factory fire leaves seven dead and fifty missing; Nepal's prime minister meets Modi in New Delhi; and India post a record Test total of 720/9 in Wellington. Drawn as a one-page newspaper front page — masthead, lead story, and six verified stories with their datelines.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The program copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to the output directory. `design.html` is a human-readable rendering of the same front page; `main.ts` is the entrypoint the grader runs.
